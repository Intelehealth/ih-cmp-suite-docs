sso_login

POST 
/api/v1/admin/operator/sso_login

SA-06: single sign-on through the organisation's OpenID Connect provider.

The console completes the provider's login and posts the id_token here. It is verified against the provider's published keys (SSO_JWKS_URL), its issuer (SSO_ISSUER) and this CMS as audience (SSO_AUDIENCE); its email must belong to an ACTIVE operator. When the provider attests MFA (amr claim) the session is MFA-verified; otherwise an account with TOTP enrolled must still pass verify_mfa.

Requires permission operator:write.
